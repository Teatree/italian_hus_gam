About Guess My Casa

Guess My Casa is a free daily house price guessing game. Every day one real home that is up for sale goes on the board. Most are in Italy, with the odd one in Spain, Greece or Finland.

The price you are guessing is the real asking price from the listing. Once the game is over you can open the listing and look around the house yourself.

A new casa goes up every day at 10:00 Kyiv time. When you are done, you can share your result without giving the price away.

How to play
  1. Look at the photo and the clue, then guess the asking price of today's casa.
  2. You get 6 tries. Land within 10% of the price and you win.
  3. Miss, and an arrow points the way: ↑ go higher, ↓ go lower. Two arrows mean you are more than 30% off.
  4. Every miss opens one more photo and one more clue.
  5. Come back tomorrow. A new casa goes up every day at 10:00 Kyiv time.
